Buyer's Simple Material Guide

Understanding Plywood Grades: MR, BWR & BWP

Choosing the right plywood ensures your furniture lasts for decades without swelling or termite damage. We work strictly with the grade and brand you choose.

IS:303 • MR Grade Dry Indoor Use

MR Plywood (Moisture Resistant)

Also known as Commercial Plywood. It resists normal indoor humidity and dampness, making it economical and strong for dry rooms.

Best Used For:

Bedroom Wardrobes, Hydraulic Beds, TV Units, Study Tables, Bookshelves & Living Room Partition.

IS:303 • BWR Grade Semi-Wet Areas

BWR Plywood (Boiling Water Resistant)

Bonded with phenol-formaldehyde resin, BWR plywood withstands water splashes, steam, and seasonal monsoon moisture without delaminating.

Best Used For:

Upper Kitchen Cabinets, Loft Storage, Utility Cabinets, Dining Units & Balcony-Facing Rooms.

IS:710 • BWP Marine 100% Waterproof

BWP Plywood (Boiling Water Proof)

Highest marine-grade waterproof & termite-proof plywood. Built to handle direct water contact, heavy loads, and humid coastal climates.

Best Used For:

Under-Sink Kitchen Cabinets, Lower Kitchen Trolleys, Bathroom Vanities & Damp Exterior Walls.
