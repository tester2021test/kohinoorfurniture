Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know before you begin.

Do you provide work with material and without material?

Yes. Kohinoor Furniture offers both options. Share your project details on WhatsApp to discuss the scope, material responsibilities and quotation.

Which areas do you serve?

Our stated service areas are Ambernath, Kalyan, Thane and Navi Mumbai. Contact us to confirm availability for your exact site.

Do you take residential and commercial projects?

We discuss both residential and commercial furniture and interior requirements. The exact work scope is confirmed for each project.

How can I request a quote?

Use any WhatsApp enquiry link on this page. Include your location, type of work, approximate dimensions, reference photos if available, and whether you prefer with-material or without-material work.

Can I share my room photos or design reference?

Yes. Send photos, measurements or inspiration images directly in the WhatsApp chat to help explain your requirements.
